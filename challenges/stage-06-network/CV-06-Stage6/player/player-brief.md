# CV-06 — The Final Trace

**Domain:** Networking / packet analysis  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Tools:** Wireshark

## Scenario

The CyberVault investigation has recovered a network capture from the period of the employee-account incident. The capture contains routine internal activity and may also contain evidence of an unauthorized transfer.

## Your task

Analyze `final-trace.pcap`, identify the suspicious communication, inspect the relevant conversation, and recover the final investigation flag. Submit the flag using the method provided by your instructor.

All addresses and traffic in this file are synthetic and created for this lab. Do not scan or connect to any address found in the capture.
