# CV-04 — The Employee Evidence

**Domain:** Digital Forensics  
**Difficulty:** Moderate  
**Suggested tools:** Autopsy and standard forensic command-line tools

## Scenario

During the CyberVault incident investigation, the security team recovered a small evidence package from an employee onboarding workstation and a removable device. The records cover the investigation window listed in the case notes.

## Your task

Examine the supplied evidence, correlate relevant users, timestamps, files, and device activity, then recover the incident flag. Preserve the supplied evidence and perform your analysis on a working copy. Submit the flag using the method provided by your instructor.

All records and identifiers are synthetic and were created for this lab. Do not connect to or investigate systems outside the supplied materials.
